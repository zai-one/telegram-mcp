# Digest rules

- Order: Needs reply -> FYI -> Noise/channels; inside a group newest first.
- One line per chat: title, unread count, who wants what, since when.
- "Needs reply": direct questions, money, deadlines, meetings, @mentions of the user.
- Quote at most one short phrase per chat; never paste whole messages or phone numbers.
- End with: "Nothing was marked as read." and the number of chats skipped (ACL or limit).
- Language: answer in the user's language.
